# Acme Corp Remote Work Policy (sample document)

## Eligibility

Employees who have completed their 3-month probation period may work remotely
up to 3 days per week. Remote work must be approved by the employee's manager.

## Equipment

The company provides a laptop and a one-time home office allowance of 300 EUR.
Employees are responsible for a stable internet connection of at least 50 Mbit/s.

## Working hours

Core hours are 10:00 to 15:00 in the employee's local time zone. Outside core
hours, employees may schedule their work flexibly.

## Security

Company data must only be accessed over the corporate VPN. Public Wi-Fi may be
used only with the VPN enabled. Lost or stolen devices must be reported to the
IT helpdesk within 24 hours.
